Guides

Practical, step-by-step tutorials with working inputs and real outputs: getting public web data into spreadsheets, Slack and AI tools.

  • LinkedIn job alerts in Slack without logging in to LinkedIn
    Get a Slack message with only the new LinkedIn jobs for your keywords and country, every morning, without a LinkedIn account or cookies. Working input, real output and the Python version.
  • Get recently funded startups into a Google Sheet every day, from SEC Form D filings
    A daily Google Sheet of US companies that just reported raising money (amount, industry, state, website), built from official SEC Form D filings with a short Python script.
  • Track Amazon New Releases daily and catch new products as they enter the list
    Save Amazon's Hot New Releases top 100 for any category every day, flag the products that just entered, and spot new launches with few reviews, with a short Python script.
  • Toronto development applications for contractors - a weekly list of new projects from open data
    Pull new Toronto rezoning, site plan and condominium applications (address, ward, storeys, units) from the City's open data every week, filter them for your trade, and get them into a spreadsheet.
  • Export Google Maps search results to CSV or Google Sheets, for many cities at once
    Turn "roofing contractor in Calgary" style Google Maps searches into a spreadsheet with phone, website, rating, review count, hours and coordinates, across many cities in one run, without the Places API or a Google account.
  • Find a competitor's longest-running Facebook and Instagram ads with the Meta Ad Library
    Pull every active ad a brand runs from the Meta Ad Library, sort by how long each has been running, and export the copy, landing pages and formats of the ones they keep paying for. No Facebook login or API token.
  • Compare more than 5 keywords in Google Trends on one scale, and export them to CSV
    Google Trends compares at most 5 terms at a time. Here is how to put 50 or 500 keywords on one shared 0-100 scale with an anchor keyword, and export interest over time, top regions and rising queries to a spreadsheet.
  • Turn a documentation site into one Markdown file for ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor
    Crawl a docs site, strip menus, footers and cookie banners, and merge every page into a single Markdown file (or an llms-full.txt) you can upload to ChatGPT, Claude, a Claude Project or Cursor.
