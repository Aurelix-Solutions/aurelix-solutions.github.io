FAQ

Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the City of Toronto's Open Data portal: the building permit applications and development applications datasets, used under the Open Government Licence – Toronto. Every row links back to its City record.

Is it really free?

Yes. The first 4 weeks are free. After that it's $29/month if you want to keep it. No card is needed now.

How do I unsubscribe?

Reply "stop" to any email, or write to aurelix_solutions@outlook.com, and we'll take you off the list.

Is personal data included?

No. The file lists projects, not people: no owner names, phone numbers or emails. Only what the City publishes about each application.
